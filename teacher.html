LO Information

Teachers only need to enter the LO number. For example, LO Number 4 will become PRO_LO4.

Answer Bullets

Enter one answer bullet per line. These are the teacher-approved canonical answers.

Enter one hint per line, matching each answer bullet. Example:
[Reacting group] Identify the first functional group involved.
[Reacting group] Identify the second functional group involved.
[Product] State what bond is formed.

Keywords, Accepted Variants & Unacceptable Answers

Enter one keyword per row. Accepted variants and unacceptable answers are optional.
For multiple variants or unacceptable answers, separate them using semicolons.
